Hoplanet

Circle a planet. Tap to let go. Land in the next orbit.

One-tap arcade for Android Coming to Google Play

How it plays

  1. Circle

    Your ball orbits a planet on its own. There is nothing to steer.

  2. Let go

    Tap, and it flies off in a straight line, exactly where it was heading.

  3. Land

    Reach the next planet’s orbit and it catches you. Miss, and the round is over.

Hit the sweet spot for a PERFECT and chain them into a combo. Fly past a planet to the one after it and the jump is worth more. The further you go, the smaller the orbits get, and some planets start to move.
